# Fortune in a Pot

Entry for [Fortune in a Pot](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9)
on make.fun — a single line, at most 120 characters, telling a houseplant's
fortune: kind, funny, and unmistakably about the plant on the windowsill.

## The fortune (116 characters)

> Soon you'll lean so far toward the sun that your keeper will turn your pot, whisper 'show-off,' and water you twice.

## Why this line

- **A fortune, not a slogan:** it opens with "Soon you'll..." — a prediction
  told to the plant itself, with the cadence of a fortune.
- **This plant, this window:** leaning toward the sun and the keeper turning
  the pot could only happen to the plant on the windowsill — not any plant
  in any room.
- **A joke that lands:** the keeper whispering "show-off" to a plant
  leaning toward the light is the kind of small joke a plant lover laughs
  at, not a pun groaned at.
- **Kind throughout:** no doom, no withering, no mockery — the reward for
  leaning toward the sun is being watered twice.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the fortune to
`/output/entry.txt`. Run locally with:

```
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
